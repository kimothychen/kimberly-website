02 About

Summary:

I’m a builder based in New York City by day and a multi-interest enthusiast by night (more on that later). I lead Business Operations at Allium across Product Marketing, Product Ops, Growth, Internal Ops, Finance, Legal, and more. Before that, I spent a few years in Finance at Blackstone on the LP Communications team and then at Pantera Capital, where I was the right hand to the Head of Investor Relations, helped build out the post-sales function, and raised a bunch of money along the way. Pantera is where I got interested in operating and building!
